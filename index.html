Privacy Policy

What LinkFlow does with your data, in plain language.

Applies to the current LinkFlow release. The same text is available inside the app under Settings → Privacy policy.

On this page Summary Data stored on your device Network connections Data sharing Permissions Logging and security Children Your choices Changes and contact

Summary

LinkFlow is designed to work on your device. We do not operate an account system, advertising network, analytics service, or developer backend that receives your data.

The app connects directly to the websites you choose, so those sites receive your IP address and normal request information under their own privacy policies. The app may also contact GitHub to download updates for its media extraction engine; that request contains no personal profile or download history.

Data stored on your device

The following data is kept locally, normally in app-private storage:

  • Download history, notes, watermark text, vault metadata, and settings.
  • Media files and exported documents saved to Downloads/LinkFlow, where you and your file manager can open them.
  • Secure Vault files, encrypted in app-private storage. The app removes the public copy after the vault copy is verified.
  • Temporary summary and clip working files in the app cache.
  • Cookies or local site data created by the built-in WebView browser while you visit sites.
  • Nearby-transfer pairing details, shown only on the two devices. They are not sent to us.
  • Watermarked videos processed on the device before saving. No frames or media are uploaded.

Android backup is disabled for LinkFlow's history, settings, and vault data. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes that local data. Files already saved to Downloads are not deleted by uninstalling the app.

Network connections

When you analyze, summarize, or download a link, LinkFlow requests the source website and the media servers it redirects to. Those services may log your IP address, user agent, and requested URL according to their own policies.

LinkFlow refreshes its extraction engine from GitHub (github.com and api.github.com) so that changing sites keep working. The request downloads a public yt-dlp release and does not include your history or account information.

LinkFlow does not import cookies from other apps, does not sign in to services, and does not use cookies to bypass access controls. The built-in browser may keep its own site data as described above.

No advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution SDK is included in the app.

Data sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data. The only network recipients are the sites you ask the app to access and GitHub for extraction-engine updates. If you email support, your message and email address are used only to respond to you.

Permissions

  • Internet and network state: analyze links, download files, and refresh the extraction engine.
  • Notifications: show download progress and completion.
  • Foreground data sync: keep an active download running reliably.
  • Wi-Fi and nearby devices: discover another LinkFlow device and transfer files directly on the local network.
  • Storage write (Android 7–9 only): save files to the public Downloads folder.
  • Biometric or device credentials: unlock Secure Vault. Authentication is handled by Android and is not sent to the developer.

Logging and security

LinkFlow avoids writing full URLs, query tokens, cookies, or authorization values to logs. Technical errors are reduced to safe user-facing messages.

Vault keys are protected by the Android Keystore. The app does not upload vault files, keys, or titles. Cleartext HTTP is disabled, and direct downloads require HTTPS.

No system is perfectly secure. Keep backups of important files and use a device lock screen.

Children

LinkFlow is not directed to children under 13. Do not use the app if you are below the minimum age required by your local law to consent to digital services.

Your choices

  • Delete individual history items, notes, clips, and downloaded files inside the app or with your file manager.
  • Clear app data or uninstall LinkFlow to remove app-private history, settings, and vault files.
  • Keep using Secure Vault or watermarking only if you want those features.
  • Use Wi-Fi-only mode to avoid mobile-data downloads.
  • Email martinguptil5392@gmail.com with a privacy question.

Changes and contact
