What LinkFlow provides

LinkFlow is a local media manager. It can analyze links to publicly available media, show quality options, save files to Downloads/LinkFlow, create clips, generate summaries from available captions or descriptions, and transfer files between your devices on the same local network.

The app runs on your device and connects directly to the website that hosts the media. LinkFlow has no accounts, advertising, or developer-operated storage server.
