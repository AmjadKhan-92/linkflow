Reliable downloads

Direct HTTPS downloads and a built-in extraction engine, with pause, resume, retry, progress notifications, and an optional Wi-Fi-only mode.

Clip and summarize

Cut timestamp clips from saved media, or generate an on-device summary from captions, falling back to the title and description when captions are missing.

🔒

Library and vault

Keep a searchable library with notes, move sensitive files into an encrypted vault, and clean up old downloads automatically.

📡

Nearby transfer

Send a file directly to another LinkFlow device on the same Wi-Fi using a six-digit pairing code. Nothing is uploaded to a server.

What LinkFlow does not do

The app is deliberately limited. It is not a way around access controls.

  • No DRM bypass. Protected streams and encrypted services are not supported.
  • No logins or paywalls. Private, age-restricted, login-only, and region-locked content is blocked.
  • No central storage. LinkFlow does not host or keep a copy of your files.
  • No tracking SDKs. There are no advertising, analytics, crash-reporting, or attribution libraries.
  • No guarantee of site support. A site can change its pages or protection at any time.
  • No cloud summary. Summaries are extractive and run on the device; the app does not transcribe audio.
  • No cloud backup of app data. Android backup is disabled for history, settings, and vault data.
  • No warranty. The app is provided as is; see the terms for details.

Boring about privacy, on purpose

Short version: your history, notes, settings, and vault stay in app-private storage. The app connects to the sites you ask it to open and to GitHub when refreshing its extraction engine. It never sends your download history or files to us. Read the full privacy policy.

Before you download

Only save what you are allowed to save. Use LinkFlow for content you own, content you created, content licensed to you, or public media that your local law and the source site's terms clearly permit. Every download requires your confirmation, and you remain responsible for the file afterwards.
